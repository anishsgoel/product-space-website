What is product management?

Product management guides a product from idea to launch and beyond. Sitting at the intersection of customer needs, technology, and business strategy, PMs define what to build and why.

User research

Learn what users actually need and where the product currently falls short.

Product strategy

Set a clear vision that grounds every execution.

Roadmapping

Prioritize features and decide what actually gets built next.

Team coordination

Work with engineers, designers, and business teams to ship meaningful ideas

How We Work

Every member moves through two sequential stages: fellowship and training, then applied client work.

1

Fellowship

A 10-week intensive training program covering product fundamentals, AI and LLM products, data and analytics, and more. Culminating in a final capstone project pitched to industry professionals.

See the curriculum
2

Client Work

Students apply what they've learned through semester long projects for real clients. Covering all services from GTM strategy to feature redesign execution.
