About Product Space

We are a club of future product leaders striving to create a community at Georgetown where students can learn about the rapidly evolving product space, hone their leadership skills, and build products end-to-end, zero-to-one.

Product management is the discipline of deciding what a company should build and why. It bridges user needs, business goals, and engineering feasibility, turning an idea into a product people actually want.

  • Learn and Grow

    Master product management through hands-on experience with real tech companies

  • Build Zero-to-One

    Create innovative products from ideation to launch with industry partners

  • Lead Tomorrow

    Develop leadership skills essential for the future of product management

How We Work

Every member moves through two sequential stages: training first, then applied client work.

1

Fellowship

A 10-week intensive training program covering product fundamentals, AI and LLM products, data and analytics, and more.

See the curriculum
2

Client Work

Fellowship graduates apply what they've learned on real engagements for companies and organizations, building products zero-to-one.
