Who We Are

What We Are

Product Space is a student-run product management consultancy at Georgetown University. We train students in product thinking through a hands-on fellowship, then put that training to work on real client engagements.

The result: students who have shipped real products, and companies who get sharp, low-risk product work from a team trained to build zero-to-one.
