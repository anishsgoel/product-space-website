Our Work

A sample of the product work our teams take on.

  1. Google

    Gemini-powered programmable search

    • AI
    • RAG
    • Ad tech

    Working with the Search Ads and Syndication team, we integrated Gemini into Programmable Search Engine so publishers could offer smarter search on their own sites.

    • Intent detection that understands what a reader is actually looking for
    • RAG context pipelines that pull the right article content into results
    • Smart ad sections that serve relevant ads to publishers without user data and without clickbait
  2. Uber

    Sizing the campus dining opportunity

    • Market research
    • Strategy

    We assessed whether the campus dining point-of-sale market was worth Uber entering, given the constraints of its hardware.

    • Stakeholder analysis with Georgetown Dining, Aramark, and Grubhub
    • Market research on how campus dining systems are bought, run, and replaced
    • A clear go or no-go recommendation grounded in Uber's hardware limits
  3. The Washington Post

    A mobile app redesigned for Gen Z

    • Product design
    • User research

    With younger readers getting their news from social media, we redesigned the Post's mobile app around how Gen Z actually discovers and reads stories.

    • Research into where and how Gen Z readers find news
    • A reading experience built for short, social-native formats
    • Concepts and prototypes for a more mobile-first Post
