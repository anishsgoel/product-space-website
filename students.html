Product Fellowship

Our 10-week intensive fellowship program equips students with comprehensive product management skills, with a focus on AI-native product development and technical fluency

  1. 01

    Product Fundamentals

    Master core PM principles, PRDs, roadmapping, and product strategy frameworks

  2. 02

    AI & LLM Products

    Build with AI APIs, prompt engineering, and understand LLM capabilities and limitations

  3. 03

    Data & Analytics

    SQL fundamentals, product metrics, A/B testing, and data-driven decision making

  4. 04

    Problem Discovery

    User research, customer interviews, and validating problems worth solving

  5. 05

    Design & Prototyping

    Wireframing, Figma prototypes, and UI/UX best practices

  6. 06

    Technical Foundations

    APIs, system design basics, Git, and working effectively with engineering teams

What You'll Get

Fellowship graduates apply their training on real client engagements, build a portfolio of zero-to-one work, and join a network of alumni at top companies.

  • Real Client Work

    Apply what you learn directly on real engagements for companies and organizations

  • A Community

    Join a network of peers, board members, and alumni working in product

  • A Portfolio

    Leave with concrete, shippable work to show for your time in the club

  • An Alumni Network

    Tap into graduates now working in product at top companies
