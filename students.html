Product Fellowship

Our 10-week intensive fellowship program equips students with comprehensive product management skills, with a focus on AI-native product development and technical fluency

  1. Product Fundamentals

    Master core PM principles, PRDs, roadmapping, and product strategy frameworks

  2. AI & LLM Products

    Build with AI APIs, prompt engineering, and understand LLM capabilities and limitations

  3. Data & Analytics

    SQL fundamentals, product metrics, A/B testing, and data-driven decision making

  4. Problem Discovery

    User research, customer interviews, and validating problems worth solving

  5. Design & Prototyping

    Wireframing, Figma prototypes, and UI/UX best practices

  6. Technical Foundations

    APIs, system design basics, Git, and working effectively with engineering teams
